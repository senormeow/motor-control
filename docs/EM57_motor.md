# Nissan Leaf EM57 Motor

IPM (Interior Permanent Magnet) synchronous motor, 4 pole pairs (8 poles), from 2013+
Nissan Leaf AZE0/ZE1. Rated 80-110 kW depending on inverter; peak torque 250-320 Nm;
max RPM 10,500 (original Leaf inverter). Strong magnetic saliency (Lq > Ld) supports
reluctance torque under MTPA. Empirical voltage constant ~0.062 V/RPM peak-phase,
~16 mech RPM per volt of available phase voltage.

## Motor overview
- Permanent magnet synchronous motor (IPM — Interior Permanent Magnet)
- 4 pole pairs (8 poles)
- 48 stator slots (producing 48 cogging detents per mechanical revolution)
- Strong magnetic saliency (Lq > Ld), supports reluctance torque
- Rated ~80 kW continuous, ~250 Nm continuous, ~350 Nm peak
- Rated stator current ~400 A; peak ~700 A
